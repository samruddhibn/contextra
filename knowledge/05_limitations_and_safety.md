# Limitations, privacy and safety

CONTEXTRA is a research prototype and an advisory system. It does not control a vehicle and is not a certified safety product.

The fusion model is trained on synthetic scenario data generated by an expert-knowledge simulator, because no public dataset links these combined inputs to real accident outcomes. Reported accuracy therefore shows that the pipeline works on that data, not that it predicts real crashes. Eye closure, yawning and head pose are indicators of possible fatigue or distraction, not proof of either.

Face detection and all feature extraction run locally. Video frames are not stored or uploaded; only small numeric features are exchanged between components. Thresholds are tuned per person by a short calibration but have not been validated on a labelled drowsiness dataset such as NTHU-DDD. Safety-relevant outputs come from the deterministic model and thresholds; the language model only explains them.
