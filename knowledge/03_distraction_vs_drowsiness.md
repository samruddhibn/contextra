# Distraction versus drowsiness

Drowsiness and distraction are different risks. A drowsy driver shows gradually longer eye closures, yawning and nodding. A distracted driver may be fully alert but has their head turned away from the road, for example while talking to a passenger or using a phone.

CONTEXTRA measures distraction as head yaw away from the driver's calibrated neutral direction. The distraction fraction is the share of the last minute during which the head was turned beyond the limit. Because the consequence of looking away depends on how fast the vehicle is moving, distraction is weighted more heavily at higher speed.

The system warns quickly about sustained head turn and sustained eye closure, and uses the fusion model to judge the overall risk once vehicle and road context are included.
